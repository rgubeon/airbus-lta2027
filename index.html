More than a conference. An experience that connects, inspires and evolves.

01 — THE PURPOSE1 of 6

Showcasing the C295 and CN235

Highlight the strengths of both aircraft platforms, showcasing their capabilities, evolution and potential to meet future operational challenges. A conference that reflects the identity, values and excellence of Airbus Defence and Space.

02 — THE CREATIVE CONCEPT2 of 6

A Narrative Focused on the Future

Develop a creative concept that becomes the guiding thread of the conference, bringing its message of evolution, innovation and new opportunities to life through the visual identity, event spaces and overall experience.

03 — THE EXPERIENCE3 of 6

From Attendees to Active Participants

Foster an engaging and interactive conference where operators can ask questions, share experiences, expand their knowledge and build new connections. An experience that strengthens trust, a sense of community and long-term relationships with Airbus.

Design a spatial and experiential environment that enhances the conference structure defined by Airbus: plenary sessions, working groups and marketplaces. Connected, functional and inspiring spaces that encourage interaction and give each format the visibility it deserves.

One Team. One Shared Vision.

Work alongside Airbus from day one, combining its expertise with beon.'s experience in creativity, design and production. A partnership built on trust, anticipation and a shared ambition to take each edition to the next level.

06 — OUR COMMITMENT6 of 6

Sustainable Innovation and Efficiency

Optimise resources from the initial design stage, prioritising the reuse of elements, modular solutions and digitalisation. A responsible, efficient conference designed to evolve and improve with every future edition.

05 · Social Event · June 2 · 20:00 – 22:30

One evening, three ways to discover Seville.

Choose a proposal to discover it.

05 · Social Event · June 2 · 20:00 – 22:30

Proposal 1 of 3

The privilege of history.

An exclusive experience at the Reales Atarazanas of Seville.

The Reales Atarazanas of Seville, one of the city's most remarkable heritage treasures, will provide the setting for a truly unique experience for Airbus guests.

Following its recent restoration and transformation into a new cultural venue, this iconic 13th-century building will exceptionally open its doors to host our event.

The experience will begin with a private guided tour, offering guests the opportunity to discover the history, architecture and extraordinary restoration process of this former medieval shipyard.

This will be followed by a cocktail-style dinner in a magnificent historic setting, designed to encourage interaction and networking in an exclusive atmosphere.

A rare and privileged opportunity to discover and experience one of Seville's most extraordinary landmarks before it officially opens to the general public.

Proposal 2 of 3

Triana, the soul of Seville.

Discovering the authentic spirit of Seville.

An exclusive guided walking tour through Triana, one of Seville's most iconic and authentic neighbourhoods.

Guests will discover its charming streets, rich history and cultural heritage, exploring the traditions that have shaped its unique identity, from its renowned ceramic craftsmanship to its deep connection with flamenco.

Divided into eight groups of 25 participants, guests will follow carefully planned routes, allowing them to enjoy the experience comfortably while discovering the true essence of Triana.

The evening will conclude with an elegant dinner at Abades Triana, overlooking the Guadalquivir River and Seville's spectacular historic skyline.

Proposal 3 of 3

The spirit of Seville's Fair.

Experience the magic of the Feria de Abril.

An exclusive Andalusian experience at Hacienda Los Ángeles, where guests will discover the authentic spirit of Seville's famous April Fair.

A festive evening filled with traditional decorations, live sevillanas, local gastronomy and the vibrant atmosphere of a traditional caseta.

A unique opportunity to immerse international guests in one of Seville's most iconic cultural celebrations, combining tradition, entertainment and an unforgettable dining experience.
